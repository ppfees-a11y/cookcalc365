Privacy Policy

How CookCalc365 handles calculator inputs, browser technology and advertising.

Overview

CookCalc365 provides free browser-based kitchen calculators. The amounts, units and ingredients entered into these calculators are processed by JavaScript in your browser. The site does not require you to register or provide a name, email address or payment details to use the tools.

Calculator inputs

The calculator code performs arithmetic locally in the browser. We do not design these calculators to submit your ingredient quantities or recipe amounts to our server. Basic hosting infrastructure may nonetheless process technical requests needed to deliver the pages.

Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages and related delivery infrastructure. As with ordinary websites, infrastructure providers may process information such as IP addresses, browser details, requested URLs and timestamps for security, delivery and diagnostics under their own policies.

Advertising and cookies

We use or intend to use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertising subject to applicable law, user choices and Google policies. Advertising availability and consent choices may vary by region. Learn more at Google’s advertising privacy resources linked below.

Third-party resources

Following a link to an external reference or advertiser opens a separate website governed by its own privacy practices. We do not control the content or data handling of third-party destinations.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The current version will be published on this page. Because this site does not offer user accounts or a contact service, visitors should review the policy periodically for updates.

Google privacy information

For information about Google advertising technology and controls, see Google’s advertising technology explanation.
