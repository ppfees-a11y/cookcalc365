Cooking Conversion Guide

Recipes can use U.S. customary units, metric units, or a mixture. Correct conversion is useful when a recipe comes from another country or your measuring tools use different units.

Volume Is Not Weight

A cup measures volume. Grams and scale ounces measure weight or mass. A cup of flour does not weigh the same as a cup of sugar or water, so accurate cup-to-gram conversion requires ingredient-specific information.

Using a Kitchen Scale

When a recipe specifies weight, a kitchen scale can reduce variation caused by packing an ingredient more or less tightly into a measuring cup. This is particularly useful in baking.

Fahrenheit and Celsius

To convert Fahrenheit to Celsius, subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9. To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply by 9/5 and add 32. Household ovens can vary from their selected setting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a U.S. cup exactly 240 mL?

No. A U.S. customary cup is approximately 236.59 mL. Some recipes round to 240 mL for convenience.

Are fluid ounces the same as ounces by weight?

No. A fluid ounce measures volume. An ounce on a kitchen scale measures weight.

Can I convert cups directly to grams?

Not accurately without identifying the ingredient, because ingredients have different densities.

Does this use U.S. or Imperial fluid ounces?

The volume converter uses U.S. customary fluid ounces. Imperial measurements differ.

Should recipe conversions be rounded?

It depends on the recipe. Everyday cooking may tolerate small differences, while baking and small quantities can benefit from greater precision.

Is the oven conversion exact?

The mathematical conversion is direct, but actual household oven temperatures can vary from the selected setting.
